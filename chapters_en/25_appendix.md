## Appendix A. Emergency card (Save to your phone) 

*Read this text out loud or to yourself if you feel a sudden surge of anxiety or unsteadiness on the street:* 

1. **STOP.** Stop. Don't run. Stopping is a physiological signal to your brain: *“There is no danger, we are not running away from a predator”*. 
2. **THIS IS ADRENALINE.** The pulse increased, and the picture swam because the adrenal glands released a hormone. This is normal chemistry. It will **burn completely in 3-5 minutes** if you don't start scaring yourself with thoughts. Wait. 
3. **SQUARE BREATHING:** Inhale (4 seconds) ➔ hold your breath (4 seconds) ➔ exhale (4 seconds) ➔ hold your breath (4 seconds). Do 5 such cycles to equalize the balance of oxygen and carbon dioxide. 
4. **GROUNDING 5-4-3-2-1:** 
* 👁 Find with your eyes **any 5 blue or green objects** around you. 
* ✋ Become aware of **4 tactile sensations** (how your feet press on your soles, how the wind touches your cheeks, how your fingers squeeze your keys/phone). 
* 👂 Hear **3 different sounds** (the hum of cars, a bird, someone talking). 
* 👃 Feel **any 2 smells** (the smell of coffee, asphalt, your jacket). 
* 👅 Feel **1 taste** (take a sip of water). 
5. **LOOK AT THE HORIZON:** Raise your chin. Shift your gaze to a distant building or tree. Don't look at your feet! 
6. **PARADOXICAL SURRENDER:** Tell fear: *“Come on, spin me around even more. I allow this to happen. I'm standing here, I'm safe."*

## Appendix B: How to Measure Progress (HADS and DHI Tests) 

To prevent your frightened brain from devaluing the results, translate your progress into the language of numbers. 

### 1. Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS) 
* **Norm:** 0–7 points. 
* **Subclinical anxiety/depression:** 8–10 points. 
* **Clinically significant anxiety/depression:** 11 points and above. 
* *Your goal:* reduce your anxiety score below 8 points. 

### 2. Dizziness Handicap Inventory (DHI) 
* **Mild degree of restrictions:** 0–30 points. 
* **Average degree of restrictions:** 31–60 points. 
* **Severe degree of restrictions:** 61–100 points. 
* *Your goal:* reducing the DHI index by 18 points or more during the period of working on the book (mathematically confirmed clinical improvement).

## Appendix E. Glossary of Key Terms 

| Term | Meaning | 
| :--- | :--- | 
| **PPPD** | Persistent postural-perceptual dizziness (ICD-11: AA36) | 
| **Amygdala** | The amygdala is the brain's fear center and triggers the fight-or-flight response | 
| **CNS** | Central nervous system | 
| **Vegetatics (VNS)** | Autonomic (autonomic) nervous system - controls automatic processes | 
| **Cute** | Section of the ANS responsible for mobilization (acceleration of the pulse, vasoconstriction) | 
| **Parasympathetic** | Department of the ANS responsible for relaxation and recovery | 
| **Cortisol** | Stress hormone secreted by the adrenal glands | 
| **Adrenaline** | Fight-or-flight hormone, burns off in 3-5 minutes | 
| **Oxytocin** | The “attachment hormone,” a cortisol antagonist, is released during self-compassion | 
| **VOR** | The vestibulo-ocular reflex is the connection between the vestibular apparatus and the eyes | 
| **CAS** | Cognitive Attentional Syndrome - internal scanner (rumination + monitoring) | 
| **CBT** | Cognitive Behavioral Therapy | 
| **MKT** | Metacognitive therapy (according to Wells) | 
| **PRT** | Pain Reprocessing Therapy - pain reprocessing therapy (according to Gordon) | 
| **SMER** | Diary: Situation → Thought → Emotion → Reaction | 
| **DHI** | Dizziness Handicap Inventory - Index of Dizziness Disorders | 
| **HADS** | Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale - anxiety and depression scale | 
| **Proprioception** | Sensation of body position in space (sensors in muscles, joints, feet) | 
| **Derealization** | Feeling of the unreality of the surrounding world (“like behind glass”, “like in a fog”) | 
| **Optical flow** | Movement of a visual image on the retina when moving | 
| **Neuroplasticity** | The brain's ability to rebuild neural connections | 
| **SSRI** | Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (antidepressants) | 
| **BDNF** | Brain-derived neurotrophic factor is a “fertilizer” for neurons |
