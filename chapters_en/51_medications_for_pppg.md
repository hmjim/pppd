# Medications for PPPD: What Actually Works (SSRIs vs Vestibular Suppressants)

When navigating chronic dizziness, patients often undergo extensive pharmacological trials—ranging from nootropics and vascular infusions to sedatives, tranquilizers, and antidepressants.

Which pharmacological agents are backed by international clinical evidence, and which ones actively delay neuroplastic recovery?

---

## 1. “Vascular” Drugs and Nootropics (Mexidol, Cavinton, Piracetam)

**Clinical Efficacy for PPPD: 0% (Evidence Grade D).**

PPPD is not caused by cerebral vascular insufficiency, “pinched vertebral arteries,” or cervical osteochondrosis. It is a functional network disorder of central sensory processing. Nootropics and vasodilator infusions have no mechanism of action on parieto-insular vestibular cortex pathways and produce only short-lived placebo effects.

---

## 2. Benzodiazepines and Vestibular Suppressants (Phenazepam, Clonazepam, Ativan)

**Hazardous for long-term management!**

Benzodiazepines temporarily suppress central nervous system excitability, blunting anxiety and acute dizziness. However:
* They are **potent vestibular suppressants**—they directly inhibit cerebellar neuroplasticity and block long-term brain compensation;
* They carry substantial risks of physiological tolerance, dependence, and severe rebound dizziness upon withdrawal.
* They should be reserved strictly for short-term acute crises, never for baseline chronic PPPD treatment.

---

## 3. The Gold Standard: SSRIs and SNRIs

According to international clinical consensus guidelines from the **Barany Society** and **Mayo Clinic**, first-line pharmacotherapy for PPPD consists of **SSRIs** (Sertraline, Escitalopram, Paroxetine) or **SNRIs** (Venlafaxine, Duloxetine).

### Therapeutic Mechanisms in PPPD:
1. **Vestibular Pathway Modulation:** They increase serotonin bioavailability in the vestibular nuclei and dorsal raphe, dampening central sensory hyper-reactivity.
2. **Limbic Down-regulation:** They attenuate chronic autonomic fight-or-flight signaling, dismantling the panic-dizziness cycle.
3. **Neuroplasticity Promotion:** They enhance Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) expression, accelerating central vestibular compensation.

*Standard therapeutic duration ranges from 6 to 12 months under the supervision of a licensed physician.*
