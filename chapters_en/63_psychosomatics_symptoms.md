# Psychosomatics: Symptoms That Doctors "Can't Find"

*You've been to ten doctors. MRI — normal. Ultrasound — normal. Blood work — normal. And you still feel like crap. Dizzy, chest pressure, lump in your throat, stomach in knots, hands shaking. And the doctor says: "Everything looks fine, try some herbal tea." Sound familiar? Then keep reading — this one's about you.*

## What Psychosomatics Actually Is — No Fluff

Psychosomatics is when your body produces real, physical symptoms in response to chronic anxiety, stress, or suppressed emotions. Not imaginary. Not "all in your head." Real — you feel them, and they're wrecking your life.

The cause just isn't a broken organ. The cause is an overloaded nervous system.

Here's an analogy. Imagine your computer is simultaneously running antivirus, disk defragmentation, a Windows update, ten Chrome tabs, and Photoshop. Every program works fine on its own. None of them are "broken." But the computer freezes, lags, and throws errors. Because the CPU is overloaded.

Your body is that same computer. Every organ works fine (your test results confirm it). But your nervous system is so overloaded that it starts "glitching" — generating symptoms that logically shouldn't exist.

## Complete List of Psychosomatic Symptoms

Here's what an overloaded nervous system can do. Not necessarily all at once — but a combo of 3–5 items from this list is a classic pattern:

### Head and Neck
- **Constant dizziness**, unsteadiness when walking — [PPPD](01_what_is_pppg.html)
- **[Brain fog](33_brain_fog.html)** — "cotton wool" feeling, heaviness, inability to concentrate
- **[Derealization](40_derealization_pppg.html)** — the world feels "behind glass," "like a movie"
- **Tension headache** — band-like pressure, squeezing in the temples, heaviness in the back of the head
- **[Lump in the throat](64_lump_in_throat.html)** — a sensation of constriction, inability to swallow

### Heart and Chest
- **[Tachycardia](67_heart_anxiety.html)** — heart "pounding" without physical exertion
- **Extrasystoles** — "skipped" beats, heart feeling like it pauses
- **Chest pain** — stabbing, pressing, "like an elephant sitting on your chest"
- **Shortness of breath** — feeling of not getting enough air, "can't take a full breath"

### Stomach
- **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)** — abdominal pain, bloating, alternating constipation and diarrhea
- **Nausea for no reason** — especially in the morning or during anxiety
- **Intestinal spasms** — stomach "twisting in knots" during stress

### Muscles and Movement
- **[Muscle armor](04_muscle_armor.html)** — chronic tension in the neck, shoulders, jaw
- **Back pain** — between the shoulder blades, lower back — with no herniated discs or protrusions
- **Tremor** — shaking hands, chin trembling, internal vibration
- **Numbness and tingling** — hands, feet, face. Like you've been sitting on a limb too long

### Nervous System
- **[Panic attacks](68_panic_attacks.html)** — episodes of sheer terror with physical symptoms
- **Insomnia** — can't fall asleep or waking up at 3 AM
- **Tinnitus** — ringing, humming, buzzing with a perfectly normal audiogram
- **Sensitivity to sounds and light** — everything irritates you, everything is "too much"
- **"Twitching eye"** — eyelid twitching (myokymia)

## Why Does the Same Stress Cause Different Symptoms?

Everyone has their own weak link. Where the chain is weakest — that's where it snaps.

One person gets stomach pain from stress. Another gets a stiff neck and dizziness. A third gets a racing heart. A fourth gets all of the above.

It depends on genetics, habitual muscle tension patterns, and medical history. If you had GI problems as a kid — anxiety will "break through" in your gut. If you once had a BPPV episode — your brain will "choose" dizziness.

But the mechanism is always the same: **chronic stress → nervous system hyperactivation → central sensitization → the body generates symptoms**.

## Central Sensitization — That's What's Going On

This is the key term. Remember it.

**Central sensitization** is when your nervous system becomes hypersensitive to EVERYTHING. Ordinary signals from your body (heartbeat, gut movements, muscle tone) get interpreted by the brain as danger.

Normal brain: "Heart beat a little harder → no big deal, move on."
Sensitized brain: "Heart beat a little harder → HEART ATTACK! CALL 911! PANIC!"

And this works with any organ. Stomach gurgles — "cancer!" Neck cracks — "my spine is falling apart!" Dizzy spell — "brain tumor!"

This is the [CAS trap](10_cas_trap.html) and the [adrenaline loop](09_adrenaline_loop.html) in their purest form.

## What to Do

Good news: if all your tests are normal — **you're not physically ill**. Your nervous system is overloaded. And it's treatable. Fully.

Bad news: it's not treatable with nootropics, herbal supplements, or "just take a vacation."

### What Works

1. **Working with anxiety** — [CBT](34_cbt_for_dizziness.html), [metacognitive therapy](15_metacognition.html)
2. **Releasing muscle tension** — [Jacobson's progressive relaxation](05_relaxation.html)
3. **Physical activity** — [exercise](13_sport.html) lowers cortisol and "discharges" the nervous system
4. **Exposure** — stop avoiding and start [living](12_exposure.html)
5. **Addressing root causes** — [why you ended up with a neurosis in the first place](17_root_causes.html)

### What Does NOT Work

- ❌ Endless testing — every new MRI only feeds health anxiety
- ❌ Nootropics and "vascular" drugs — no evidence base for psychosomatic conditions
- ❌ "Just calm down" — useless advice for an actual nervous system disorder
- ❌ Dr. Google — every symptom search will lead you to cancer or multiple sclerosis
