# Shakes when walking: reasons why your legs feel “wobbly” and what to do 

*Are you walking down the street or a shopping center, and you feel as if you are walking on foam rubber or a mattress? We explain the physiology of unsteadiness.*

---

## Why does it stagger and skid when walking? 

The feeling of **unsteadiness, unsteadiness and “wobbly legs”** when walking is one of the most common complaints in people with chronic dizziness. However, a neurological examination, MRI of the brain and blood tests often show an ideal norm. 

If unsteadiness persists for more than 3 months, and doctors do not find organic pathology, in 80% of cases we are talking about **PPPD** (persistent postural-perceptual dizziness). 

## Physiology of “cotton legs” and pitching 

In a normal state, balance is maintained by three systems: 
1. **Vision** (we see the horizon and obstacles) 
2. **Vestibular apparatus** (the inner ear determines tilts and acceleration) 
3. **Proprioception** (muscle receptors in the feet and joints sense support) 

### What happens with PPPD: 
- After suffering stress or an attack of dizziness, the brain decides that the inner ear and feet are “lying.” 
- The brain goes into **visual overprotection** mode - it tries to control every step with its eyes. 
- The muscles of the legs and neck spasm (“muscle shell”), which is why the foot receptors transmit the signal to the brain with a delay. 
- As a result, typical sensations arise: **“the ground disappears from under your feet”**, **“legs like cotton wool”**, **“swept to the side”**. 

## What kind of unsteadiness occurs when walking 

| Symptom | How does a person feel | Reason | 
| :--- | :--- | :--- | 
| **Postural unsteadiness** | Strengthens strictly while standing and on the move, lying down passes | Typical for PPPD | 
| **Visual shaky** | Drowns in supermarkets, metro, among the crowd | Visual overload in PPPD | 
| **Ataxia (cerebellar)** | Real loss of balance, falling | Organic (doctor required) | 

> **Important:** With PPPD, you only *feel* like you're going to fall. There are no real falls because the balance system is physically intact. 

## How to get rid of unsteadiness when walking 

### 1. Stop looking at your feet 
When you frantically control every step with your eyes, you feed visual addiction. Look straight ahead and trust your feet. 

### 2. Vestibular gymnastics 
Special exercises restore the brain's trust in the vestibular system and joints. More details: [Vestibular gymnastics for PPPD](06_vestibular.html). 

### 3. Relieving muscle spasm 
Chronically tight leg and neck muscles distort proprioception. Use [Jacobson Progressive Relaxation](05_relaxation.html).

### 4. Exposure (do not avoid walking) 
The more you sit at home out of fear that you will get carried away, the more your brain becomes convinced of the Danger Zone. Walk every day, gradually increasing the distance.
