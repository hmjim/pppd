# PPPD symptoms - complete list of signs 

*Detailed list of all symptoms of PPPV (persistent postural-perceptual dizziness) with explanations. If you recognize yourself in at least 3-4 points, you are in the right place.* 

## 5 diagnostic criteria for PPPD (Barany Society, 2017) 

The official criteria for diagnosing PPPD are: 

1. **Feeling of unsteadiness, unsteadiness or random dizziness** - present for **3 months or more** 
2. **Symptoms last most of the day** - at least 15 days a month. Can wax and wane in waves 
3. **Amplification with vertical position, movement and visual stimulation** - standing, walking, transport, shops, screens 
4. **The onset is preceded by a trigger event** - BPPV, vestibular neuronitis, panic attack, stress, trauma 
5. **Symptoms are not explained by another disease** - organic causes have been excluded by examination 

## Main symptoms of PPPD 

### Shakiness and instability 
- Feeling of a “floating floor” - as if you are walking on the deck of a ship 
- “Cotton” or “drunk” legs when walking 
- Feeling like the ground is disappearing from under your feet 
- Rocking while standing still, especially with eyes closed 
- Increased unsteadiness in the morning (after sleep the brain does not have time to “recalibrate” the balance) 

### Dizziness 
- Not rotational (not a carousel), but **swaying, floating, unsteadiness** 
- Increased in an upright position, decreased while lying down 
- Provocation when turning the head, bending 
- Feeling of “sinking” when walking 

### Visual symptoms 
- “Floating” picture before your eyes 
- Discomfort from flickering lights, screens, scrolling 
- Deterioration in visually rich environments - shops, shopping centers, metro 
- [Visual dependence](08_visual_dependence.html) - the brain overly relies on vision 

### Cognitive symptoms 
- [Fog in the head](33_brain_fog.html) - “cotton wool”, heaviness, lethargy 
- Difficulty concentrating 
- Feeling of “unreality” - [derealization](27_depersonalization.html) 
- Absent-mindedness, forgetfulness 

### Muscle symptoms 
- Chronic tension in the neck and shoulders - [muscle armor](04_muscle_armor.html) 
- Jaw tightness (bruxism) 
- Pain in the neck, back of the head 
- Feeling of a “hoop” on the head 

### Alarming symptoms 
- Fear of falling, loss of consciousness 
- Panic attacks - [adrenaline loop](09_adrenaline_loop.html) 
- Hyperfixation on bodily sensations 
- Obsessive “scanning” of the body 
- Avoidance of frightening places (agoraphobia) 

## What is NOT a symptom of PPPD 

If you have anything from this list, you need to be further examined:

- **Loss of consciousness** (fainting) is not PPPV 
- **True rotational vertigo** (attacks of 5-60 seconds when turning the head) is more likely to be BPPV 
- **Hearing loss in one ear** - may indicate Meniere's disease 
- **Double vision** - neurological pathology 
- **Speech or swallowing problems** - requires urgent examination 

## PPPD is completely reversible 

All of these symptoms are manifestations of a functional failure, not organic damage. With proper work ([vestibular gymnastics](06_vestibular.html), [CBT](34_cbt_for_dizziness.html), [exposure](12_exposure.html)), symptoms go away in 2–6 months.
