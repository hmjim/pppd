# Stress-Related Back and Neck Pain: When "Osteochondrosis" Is Actually Psychosomatics

*Your back hurts — so you go to a neurologist. They order an MRI, find "protrusions" and "osteochondrosis," prescribe B-vitamins and massage. A month later — nothing's changed. Why? Because the source of your pain isn't in your spine.*

## "Osteochondrosis" — The Favorite Wastebasket Diagnosis

Let's be real. In Russian-speaking medicine, "osteochondrosis" is a catch-all junk diagnosis — a term rarely used in Western medicine. Doctors dump everything into it that they can't explain: neck pain, back pain, dizziness, numb hands. "You have cervical osteochondrosis" — heard that before? So have I. About twenty times.

Here's the fact: degenerative spinal changes (the kind labeled "osteochondrosis") are found in **90% of people over 30**. People with no pain. People who run marathons. People who've never once complained about their back.

Disc protrusions on an MRI are like wrinkles on your face. Everyone has them. They are **not the cause** of your pain. They're an age-related norm.

## So Where Does the Pain Come From?

From your muscles. Specifically — from chronic muscle spasm caused by stress.

When you're under stress, your body switches to "fight-or-flight" mode. Muscles tense up — getting ready to fight or run. This is biological autopilot, millions of years old.

The problem: in modern life, stress never ends. You're not running from a tiger — you're sitting at a desk stressing about work, money, relationships, and your health. And your muscles **never relax**. Ever.

This is the [muscle armor](04_muscle_armor.html) described by Wilhelm Reich.

### Where It Locks Up

- **Neck and trapezius muscles** — the most common zone. Chronic spasm of the suboccipital muscles. Consequences: [dizziness](37_pppg_dizziness.html), [brain fog](33_brain_fog.html), tension headaches.
- **Between the shoulder blades** — the interscapular area. "Burning back," "feels like a stake driven in."
- **Lower back** — spasm of the iliopsoas muscle (psoas) — the body's primary stress muscle.
- **Jaw** — bruxism (teeth grinding in sleep). Morning result: jaw and temple pain.

### The Mechanism

1. Chronic stress → cortisol and adrenaline surge
2. Muscles receive the command "tense up" — and execute it 24/7
3. Constant spasm → impaired blood flow in the muscle → lactic acid buildup → **pain**
4. Pain → anxiety → "what's wrong with me?" → even more stress → even more spasm

Recognize that [adrenaline loop](09_adrenaline_loop.html)? It's here too.

## The Connection to PPPD and Other Symptoms

If your neck hurts and at the same time:
- You feel unsteady when walking — that's classic PPPD. A locked-up neck → distorted proprioceptive signals from neck muscles to the cerebellum → the brain "miscalculates coordinates" → a sensation of unsteadiness.
- Your head feels heavy — [muscle armor](04_muscle_armor.html) compresses venous outflow → congestion → heaviness, "fog."
- [Lump in your throat](64_lump_in_throat.html) — spasm of neck and laryngeal muscles — part of the same armor.

It's all the same thing. One overloaded nervous system. Different "error screens."

## Why Massage Doesn't Help (For Long)

Massage feels great. And you really do feel better afterward. For a few hours. Maybe a couple of days. Then the spasm comes back.

Why? Because the massage therapist relaxes the muscle **with their hands**. But the command "tense up" keeps coming **from the brain**. The brain is the commanding officer. The muscle is the soldier. Until the officer cancels the order — the soldier stays at attention.

To release chronic spasm, you need to work with the **brain**, not the muscle:

1. **[Jacobson's progressive muscle relaxation](05_relaxation.html)** — you train the brain to issue the "relax" command. This is a learnable skill. After 2–3 weeks of daily practice, the brain begins to automatically "switch off" the spasm.

2. **Lowering your anxiety level** — [CBT](34_cbt_for_dizziness.html), [metacognitive therapy](15_metacognition.html). Less stress → the brain sends the "tense up" command less often → muscles relax.

3. **Physical activity** — [exercise](13_sport.html). Cardio "burns off" adrenaline and cortisol. Stretching lengthens chronically spasmed muscles. Strength training builds proper muscle tone.

4. **Addressing the root causes of stress** — [root causes](17_root_causes.html), [suppressed emotions](28_suppressed_emotions.html). Until you figure out why you're in chronic stress — your back will keep hurting.
