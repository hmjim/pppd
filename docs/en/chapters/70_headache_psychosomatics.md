# Stress Headaches: When Your Head Feels Like It's in a Vise — But the MRI Is Clean

*Your head is splitting. Pressure in the temples. A tight band around the back of your skull. Like someone put a helmet on you two sizes too small. You got an MRI — all clear. The neurologist prescribed ibuprofen. But your head hurts every single day. What's going on?*

## Tension-Type Headache — The Most Common and Most Misunderstood

This is an official diagnosis: **tension-type headache** (TTH). According to the WHO, it affects 40% of the population. It's the most common type of headache in the world — and the most ignored.

### How it feels

- A "band" around the head — pressure along the entire circumference
- Heaviness in the back of the head and temples
- A sensation of wearing a "helmet" or "cap"
- The pain isn't pulsating — it's pressing, squeezing
- Usually bilateral (your "whole head" hurts, not just one side)
- No nausea or vomiting (that's what distinguishes it from migraine)
- No aura (flashes, zigzags in your vision)

### What is NOT a tension-type headache

- **Migraine** — pulsating, usually one-sided, with nausea and light sensitivity
- **Cluster headache** — brutal pain around one eye, attacks lasting 15–180 minutes
- **Secondary headache** — caused by a specific condition (hypertension, tumor, meningitis)

If your MRI is normal, there's no vomiting, no vision loss, no worsening morning headaches — it's most likely a tension-type headache. In other words — psychosomatic.

## Why Stress Causes Headaches

The mechanism is the same as with all psychosomatic symptoms — the [muscle armor](04_muscle_armor.html).

Chronic stress → the brain commands muscles to "tense up" → muscles of the neck, trapezius, back of the head, forehead, jaw — everything clenches → chronic spasm → impaired blood flow → pain.

And you don't even have to be "stressed out" in the moment. You could be sitting in a café, sipping coffee, chatting with a friend. But your muscles are in spasm. Because the "tense up" command is coming from the subconscious. It's become background noise. Like a laptop fan — you don't hear it, but it's always running.

### Bruxism

A separate issue — **bruxism** (teeth grinding during sleep). This is also a manifestation of stress. The jaw muscles are among the most powerful in the body. When they clench all night long — you wake up with a headache, pain in the temples, and a sore jaw.

Your dentist will say: "Get a mouth guard." The guard will protect your teeth, but it won't remove the root cause — anxiety.

## The Connection to PPPD

Tension-type headache and PPPD are twins. Both feed off the same muscle spasm in the neck.

Tight neck → compressed venous drainage → heaviness in the head → [brain fog](33_brain_fog.html).
Tight neck → distorted proprioception → unsteadiness when walking → [PPPD](01_what_is_pppg.html).
Tight neck → impaired blood flow in muscles → pressing pain → tension-type headache.

One cause — three different symptoms. That's why when you treat one — the others often go away too.

## What DOESN'T Work

- ❌ **Ibuprofen / Acetaminophen every day** — medication-overuse headache (MOH). If you take painkillers more than 10–15 days per month — they start CAUSING headaches themselves. A vicious cycle.
- ❌ **"Brain-boosting" supplements** — nootropics with no evidence base for tension-type headaches. No, not even if a doctor prescribed them.
- ❌ **Neck massage as the only treatment** — temporary relief. The "tense up" command keeps coming from the brain.

## What Works

1. **[Progressive Muscle Relaxation](05_relaxation.html)** — teach your brain to release the muscles. Daily. After 2–3 weeks, the headaches will start to fade.

2. **Neck and shoulder stretches** — gentle head tilts, rotations, trapezius stretches. 5 minutes morning and evening.

3. **Anxiety reduction** — [CBT](34_cbt_for_dizziness.html), [metacognitive therapy](15_metacognition.html). Less stress → the brain sends "tense up" commands less often → muscles relax → no headache.

4. **Cardio exercise** — [exercise](13_sport.html). 30 minutes of running or swimming "burns off" cortisol and adrenaline. After a workout, muscles relax physiologically.

5. **Sleep hygiene** — [sleep and PPPD](26_sleep.html). Sleep deprivation = elevated cortisol = muscle spasm = headache. Get 7–8 hours. No less.

6. **Mouth guard for bruxism** — if you grind your teeth. It'll protect your teeth and reduce the load on the jaw muscles.
