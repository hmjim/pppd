# Complete Overview of PPPD Treatment: The Integrated System

*A high-level synthesis of all five treatment pillars: Vestibular Rehab, CBT/MCT, Graded Exposure, Somatic Release, and Pharmacotherapy.*\n\n## The 5 Pillars of Recovery\n1. **Vestibular Rehabilitation (VRT)**: Gaze stabilization (VOR x1/x2) and balance retraining.\n2. **Metacognitive Therapy (MCT)**: Disengaging from the Cognitive Attentional Syndrome (CAS).\n3. **Graded Exposure**: Extinguishing fear-avoidance in supermarkets and crowded environments.\n4. **Somatic Down-Regulation**: Jacobson PMR, suboccipital release, and 30-minute daily aerobic walking.\n5. **Pharmacotherapy**: SSRIs/SNRIs (sertraline, venlafaxine) as neurochemical facilitators when indicated.
