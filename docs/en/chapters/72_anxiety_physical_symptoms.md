## "VSD" Doesn't Exist

Let's start with the uncomfortable truth: the diagnosis "VSD" (vegetovascular dystonia) **doesn't appear in ICD-10 or ICD-11**. It's absent from every international classification of diseases. It doesn't exist in Western medicine. It exists nowhere outside the post-Soviet medical tradition.

"VSD" is a Soviet-era artifact. A junk drawer where doctors tossed everything that didn't fit a clear clinical picture: rapid heartbeat without a heart defect, dizziness without a tumor, pain without an injury.

In reality, what hides behind "VSD" is almost always one of three things:
1. **An anxiety disorder** — generalized or panic disorder
2. **A functional disorder** — PPPD, IBS, functional pain
3. **Somatoform autonomic dysfunction** (F45.3 in ICD-10) — the same neurosis, just wrapped in medical packaging

## Why Anxiety Produces Physical Symptoms

Anxiety isn't "just feeling nervous." It's a full-blown biological response. When the brain decides you're in danger (even if the "danger" is the thought "what if I'm seriously ill?"), a cascade fires off:

**1. The amygdala activates the hypothalamus** → "battle stations!" command

**2. The hypothalamus activates the sympathetic nervous system** → the adrenal glands release adrenaline and cortisol

**3. The body switches to "fight or flight" mode:**
- Heart speeds up → [tachycardia](67_heart_anxiety.html), skipped beats
- Breathing accelerates → shortness of breath, hyperventilation, tingling in the hands
- Muscles tense up → [neck and back pain](66_back_pain_psychosomatics.html), tremor
- Blood diverts from the gut to the muscles → nausea, stomach cramps
- Pupils dilate → light sensitivity
- Sweating increases → clammy palms

**4. The brain scans the body** ([CAS trap](10_cas_trap.html)) → finds all these symptoms → interprets them as disease → even more anxiety → even more symptoms

This is the [adrenaline loop](09_adrenaline_loop.html). And it runs 24/7 when you're in chronic stress.

## Why Glycine, Tenoten, and Afobazole Don't Work

These are medications sold primarily on the Russian market and are not internationally recognized treatments for anxiety:

- **Glycine** — an amino acid. Your body already produces it. Taking extra tablets doesn't cross the blood-brain barrier in any meaningful amount. Efficacy: on par with placebo.
- **Tenoten** — a homeopathic product. Contains "ultra-low doses of antibodies" to the brain protein S-100. Not a single quality RCT confirms its efficacy. Literally — sugar pills.
- **Afobazole** — marketed as an anxiolytic, but lacks convincing evidence outside of Russia. Not approved by the FDA or EMA.

These drugs get prescribed because they're "safe" (read: they don't do anything) and because the doctor needs to write something on the prescription pad.
