# PPPD Dizziness vs. Other Balance Disorders

*Differential diagnostic comparison between PPPD, BPPV, Meniere disease, Vestibular Neuritis, and Mal de Debarquement Syndrome (MdDS).*\n\n## Comparative Diagnostic Guide\nUnlike BPPV, PPPD does not cause brief spinning spells lasting 30 seconds. Unlike Meniere disease, PPPD does not cause progressive low-frequency hearing loss or episodic drop attacks. PPPD is continuous, postural, and exacerbated by visual flow fields.
