About the Guide

“Point of Support” is a comprehensive clinical and engineering roadmap written by an author who experienced six months of severe unsteadiness, brain fog, and derealization following acute BPPV—and achieved 100% permanent recovery.

This is not a vague motivational essay. It is an applied operating manual based on international peer-reviewed protocols: Vestibular Rehabilitation (VRT), Metacognitive Therapy (MCT), Graded Exposure, and Somatic Down-Regulation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PPPD in simple terms?

PPPD is a functional neuro-vestibular disorder where the brain's central balance software becomes hyper-sensitized and miscalibrated, causing persistent sensations of rocking, floating, or unsteadiness despite normal inner ear and brain MRI scans.

Is this condition permanent or dangerous?

PPPD is completely non-dangerous, causes zero neuro-degeneration, and is fully reversible through systematic neuroplastic re-education.

How long does recovery take?

With daily commitment to the VRT, somatic, and metacognitive protocols outlined in this guide, most individuals experience substantial relief within 4–8 weeks, and full recovery within 3–6 months.
